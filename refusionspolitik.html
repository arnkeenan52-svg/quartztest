Returns & Refunds

14 dages fortrydelsesret

Som forbruger har du 14 dages fortrydelsesret, når du handler hos os. Fristen løber fra den dag, du modtager varen – eller henter den i vores Click & Collect-skab. Du behøver ikke begrunde, at du fortryder dit køb.

Undtagelse: brudt forsegling på fødevarer

Vores mel er en fødevare og leveres i forseglet emballage. Af hensyn til sundhedsbeskyttelse og hygiejne bortfalder fortrydelsesretten, hvis forseglingen brydes. Du kan derfor kun returnere poser, der er uåbnede og i original emballage.

Sådan fortryder du

Send en e-mail til hello@quartzmolle.dk med dit ordrenummer og besked om, at du ønsker at fortryde købet – senest 14 dage efter du har modtaget varen.

Returnér derefter varen til: Quartz Mølle, Suså Landevej 101, 4160 Herlufmagle – senest 14 dage efter, du har givet os besked. Du bærer ansvaret for varen under transporten, så gem kvitteringen fra fragtfirmaet. Du er også velkommen til at aflevere varen personligt på møllen efter aftale.

Bemærk: Du betaler selv alle omkostninger til returfragten, når du fortryder dit køb. Vi refunderer ikke udgiften til at sende varen retur.

Tilbagebetaling

Når vi har modtaget varen retur og kontrolleret dens stand, tilbagefører vi beløbet til det betalingsmiddel, du brugte ved købet. Tilbagebetalingen sker senest 14 dage efter, vi har modtaget din besked om fortrydelse – vi kan dog tilbageholde beløbet, indtil vi har modtaget varen retur.

Reklamationsret

Købelovens regler om reklamation gælder. Er der fejl eller mangler ved varen – for eksempel en beskadiget pose eller mel, der ikke lever op til det lovede – så kontakt os hurtigst muligt på hello@quartzmolle.dk med dit ordrenummer og gerne fotos. Ved en berettiget reklamation sender vi en ny vare uden beregning eller refunderer dit køb – inklusive rimelige returomkostninger. For fødevarer gælder reklamationsretten inden for varens holdbarhed.

Beskadiget ved levering

Er pakken synligt beskadiget ved modtagelsen, så tag billeder af emballagen og indholdet og kontakt os med det samme – så finder vi en løsning.

Klagemuligheder

Kan vi mod forventning ikke finde en løsning sammen, kan du klage til Center for Klageløsning, Nævnenes Hus, Toldboden 2, 8800 Viborg, via naevneneshus.dk.

14-day right of withdrawal

As a consumer, you have a 14-day right of withdrawal when you shop with us. The period runs from the day you receive the goods – or collect them from our Click & Collect locker. You do not need to give a reason for cancelling your purchase.

Exception: broken seal on food products

Our flour is a food product and is delivered in sealed packaging. For reasons of health protection and hygiene, the right of withdrawal lapses if the seal is broken. You can therefore only return bags that are unopened and in their original packaging.

How to cancel your purchase

Send an email to hello@quartzmolle.dk with your order number stating that you wish to cancel your purchase – no later than 14 days after receiving the goods.

Then return the goods to: Quartz Mølle, Suså Landevej 101, 4160 Herlufmagle, Denmark – no later than 14 days after notifying us. You are responsible for the goods during transport, so keep the receipt from the carrier. You are also welcome to hand in the goods in person at the mill by prior arrangement.

Please note: You pay all return shipping costs yourself when cancelling your purchase. We do not refund the cost of sending the goods back to us.

Refunds

Once we have received the returned goods and checked their condition, we will refund the amount to the payment method you used for the purchase. The refund is made no later than 14 days after we received your cancellation notice – however, we may withhold the refund until we have received the goods back.

Warranty (right of complaint)

The rules of the Danish Sale of Goods Act apply. If there is a fault or defect with the goods – for example a damaged bag or flour that does not live up to what was promised – please contact us as soon as possible at hello@quartzmolle.dk with your order number and preferably photos. If your complaint is justified, we will send a replacement free of charge or refund your purchase – including reasonable return costs. For food products, the right of complaint applies within the product's shelf life.

Damaged on delivery

If the parcel is visibly damaged upon receipt, please take photos of the packaging and contents and contact us right away – and we will find a solution.

Complaints

If, contrary to expectation, we cannot find a solution together, you can complain to the Danish Centre for Complaint Resolution (Center for Klageløsning), Nævnenes Hus, Toldboden 2, 8800 Viborg, Denmark, via naevneneshus.dk.
